Indie SaaS Landscape

~85%
Indie SaaS companies under $10M ARR
Approximately 85% of the 30,000+ SaaS companies globally have under $10M in annual recurring revenue, with the majority (60%+) under $1M ARR — these are the indie SaaS companies.
Source: Statista; Crunchbase; industry estimates
APA: Statista. (2026). SaaS Industry Statistics.
5–7
Average SaaS tools used per SMB
The average small-to-medium business uses 5–7 SaaS tools. Tech-forward SMBs use 15+. SaaS spend per employee averages $2,800/year, growing at 14% annually.
Source: BetterCloud; Blissfully SaaS Trends
APA: BetterCloud. (2025). SaaS Trends Report.

First Customer

3–16 weeks
Time to first paying customer
Time from launch to first paying customer for indie SaaS sits between 3 and 16 weeks. Faster than 3 weeks usually means a warm-network customer; beyond 16 weeks signals a positioning or offer problem.
Source: Unlock SaaS, Funnel Benchmarks
APA: Unlock SaaS. (2026). Indie SaaS Funnel Benchmarks. https://unlocksaas.com/benchmarks/
$30–$300
Indie SaaS customer acquisition cost
Customer acquisition cost for indie SaaS sits between $30 and $300 for self-serve products and $500 to $3,000 for sales-assisted tiers. Companies with strong organic/content channels often run CAC under $50.
Source: Unlock SaaS, Funnel Benchmarks
APA: Unlock SaaS. (2026). Indie SaaS Funnel Benchmarks.

Funnel Conversion

1–5%
Cold-traffic landing page conversion rate
For indie SaaS landing pages on cold traffic, a healthy conversion rate sits between 1% and 5%. Below 1% usually signals a Wrong Person problem; above 5% on cold traffic suggests warm-audience contamination.
Source: Unlock SaaS, Funnel Benchmarks
APA: Unlock SaaS. (2026). Indie SaaS Funnel Benchmarks.
40–70%
Checkout completion rate (cold traffic)
Cold-traffic checkout completion for indie SaaS sits between 40% and 70%. Below 40% means the price wasn't anchored upstream; above 70% suggests the price is too low to act as a serious anchor.
Source: Unlock SaaS, Funnel Benchmarks
APA: Unlock SaaS. (2026). Indie SaaS Funnel Benchmarks.
3–12%
Tripwire conversion rate (<$10 offers)
Cold-traffic tripwire conversion for indie SaaS sits between 3% and 12% for tripwires priced under $10. Below 3% means the offer feels like a trap; above 12% suggests tire-kickers who won't upgrade.
Source: Unlock SaaS, Funnel Benchmarks
APA: Unlock SaaS. (2026). Indie SaaS Funnel Benchmarks.

Email Marketing

30–55%
Indie SaaS founder email open rate
Engaged-list open rates for indie SaaS founder emails sit between 30% and 55%. Below 30% is almost always a deliverability issue, not a subject-line problem. Above 55% means a small, tightly curated list.
Source: Unlock SaaS, Funnel Benchmarks
APA: Unlock SaaS. (2026). Indie SaaS Funnel Benchmarks.
3–12%
Email click-through rate (of opens)
Click-through rates for indie SaaS founder emails sit between 3% and 12% of opens. Below 3% means the CTA isn't tied to a specific reader outcome.
Source: Unlock SaaS, Funnel Benchmarks
APA: Unlock SaaS. (2026). Indie SaaS Funnel Benchmarks.

Pricing & Monetization

15–25%
Optimal annual-vs-monthly discount
Optimal annual-vs-monthly discount for indie SaaS sits between 15% and 25%. Under 10% doesn't shift purchasing to annual; over 35% attracts price-sensitive customers with higher churn.
Source: Unlock SaaS, Funnel Benchmarks
APA: Unlock SaaS. (2026). Indie SaaS Funnel Benchmarks.
$9–$99
Self-serve subscription AOV range
Indie SaaS subscription average order value sits between $9 and $99 monthly for self-serve products. The Stack Slide presence on the pricing page moves AOV 30–80% more than price-point optimization alone.
Source: Unlock SaaS, Funnel Benchmarks
APA: Unlock SaaS. (2026). Indie SaaS Funnel Benchmarks.

Churn & Retention

5–12%
SMB SaaS monthly churn range
Monthly churn for indie SaaS serving SMBs sits between 5% and 12%. The headline number is misleading — cohort breakdown (paid vs free trial, ICP-fit vs ICP-miss) tells the real story.
Source: Unlock SaaS; Koji; Optif benchmarks
APA: Koji. (2026). SaaS Churn Rate Benchmarks 2026. Optif. (2026). B2B SaaS Churn Rate Benchmarks.
